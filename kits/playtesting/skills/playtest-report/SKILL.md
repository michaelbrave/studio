---
name: playtest-report
description: Turn playtest notes or a play session into a structured report with actionable findings. Use after playing or when given playtest feedback.
---

Sections, in order:
1. **Session.** Build or commit, who played, how long, what was being tested.
2. **First five minutes.** Was it clear what to do? Where did the player hesitate?
3. **What worked.** Specific moments, not general praise.
4. **Pain points and confusion.** What happened, where, and how often.
5. **Moments of delight.**
6. **Bugs.** One line each with severity (S1–S4); full details through the `bug-report` skill.
7. **Recommendations.** Each tied to a finding above, most impactful first.

Report what was observed. Mark guesses about why something happened as guesses.
