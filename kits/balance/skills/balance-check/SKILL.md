---
name: balance-check
description: Analyse game data and formulas for balance problems: outliers, broken progression, dominant strategies, runaway economies. Use when asked to check balance or tuning.
---

1. **Inputs.** Find the data files and formulas, and the design intent they implement. Without
   data, report "NOT ASSESSED — no data" rather than estimating.
2. **Outliers.** Items, units or abilities whose value-for-cost sits far from the rest. Show the
   numbers you compared.
3. **Progression.** Does power, cost or difficulty grow at the intended rate? Look for walls and
   flat spots.
4. **Dominant strategies.** Any choice that is always best makes the alternatives pointless.
5. **Economy.** Sources vs. sinks: does anything accumulate without limit?
6. **Report.** Health: HEALTHY / CONCERNS / CRITICAL / NOT ASSESSED. Then findings, each with the
   specific values involved and a suggested change with its expected effect. Do not edit the data
   unless the brief asks you to.
