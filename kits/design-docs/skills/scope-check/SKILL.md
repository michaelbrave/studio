---
name: scope-check
description: Compare a feature or milestone's current scope against its original plan and flag scope creep. Use when asked "has this grown?" or before committing to a plan.
---

1. Find the original plan (the brief, design doc, or milestone notes). If there is none, say so and
   stop: scope creep cannot be measured without a baseline.
2. List what the current work or plan contains.
3. Produce:
   - **Added** (not in the original), each with its likely cost (small / medium / large).
   - **Removed** (in the original, now dropped).
   - **Growth:** count added vs. original items.
   - **Risk:** what the additions put at risk (schedule, quality, other features).
4. Recommend what to cut or defer, closest-to-the-core-experience last. The user decides.
